# Locker Companion

**Version 2.0.0** · Android 7.0+ (API 24) · MIT License

Android companion app for [**Locker Pro**](../windows) (Windows). It lets you approve folder and app unlock requests on your PC using your phone's fingerprint sensor over your local Wi-Fi.  
No cloud, no account, no analytics.

---

## Features

- **Fingerprint Verification:** Uses Android's hardware biometric prompt (`BIOMETRIC_STRONG`) to approve PC unlock requests.
- **Enrolled Fingers Only:** If a new fingerprint is added on the phone, the linked key is automatically invalidated by Android so you simply re-link using your Master Password on the PC.
- **QR Pairing:** Scan the QR code shown by Locker Pro to link your phone on your local Wi-Fi (LAN ports TCP 58820 on PC, 58821 on phone).
- **Simple Controls:** View your connected PC, test the connection, or disconnect at any time.

---

## Install

Download the APK from the [Releases](https://github.com/inrwxyz/locker-pro/releases) page, or build from source below.

Permissions used: camera (for QR pairing), network/Wi-Fi state (to reach your PC on LAN), biometric (fingerprint), notifications/foreground service (unlock prompts), and vibrate.

---

## Pair with your PC

1. Install and open **Locker Pro** on your Windows PC (on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone).
2. In Locker Pro go to **Phone Link** and click **Pair New Phone**.
3. In **Locker Companion**, tap **Scan QR code** and scan the code.
4. Enter your Master Password on the PC and confirm with your fingerprint on the phone once to link.

---

## Build from source

Requirements: JDK 17+, Android SDK Platform 35 (Android Studio recommended).

```powershell
git clone https://github.com/inrwxyz/locker-pro.git
cd locker-pro\android
.\gradlew.bat assembleDebug          # APK: app\build\outputs\apk\debug\app-debug.apk
.\gradlew.bat installDebug           # build and install on an adb-connected phone
```

`local.properties` (pointing to your SDK path) is generated by Android Studio, or you can create it manually with `sdk.dir=<path to Android SDK>`.

---

## Notes

- Your phone and PC must be on the same local network for fingerprint unlock. Your PC Master Password always works offline.
- Always keep your Master Password and Recovery Key safe on the PC side.
